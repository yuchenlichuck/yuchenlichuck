## 👋 About Me

I'm a 4th-year PhD student in Computer Vision at **MBZUAI** (Abu Dhabi), working in the **Metaverse Lab** under the supervision of [Prof. Hao Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NFeigSoAAAAJ&hl=en). I work on **vision-language models (VLMs) and generative models for video and 3D**. Before that, my work on 3D vision (PointNeXt, 3D CoMPaT) was published at NeurIPS and ECCV.

I have done research internships at **Amazon Science (Prime Video)** and **Sony AI**, and I serve as a reviewer for CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, AAAI, TPAMI, IJCV, TCSVT, and BMVC.
